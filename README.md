# Relaxation

A VR game where the point is to not have a point.

You live in **Kaisei** — a warm, futuristic city at permanent golden hour. Ride the train. Eat ramen. Fish the canals. Pet the cat. And when you want intensity, walk to it: a sword-combat dojo up a side stair, or an AR layer over the whole city that you open by raising a handheld device.

- **The city** is the game. No quests, no levels, no timers.
- **STEEL GARDEN** — physics-driven VR sword combat, entered through a dojo in Low Market.
- **DRIFT** — an in-fiction AR game played through a handheld device, turning the city itself into content.
